# Authority model

The final public case study should distinguish:

- the authority that owns or governs a vault;
- the authority that represents a user or delegated action;
- the signer that explicitly authorizes a transaction;
- program-derived state that enforces relationships rather than holding a private key;
- read-only observers and infrastructure operators.

Use conceptual labels such as `governance authority`, `vault state`, `user state` and `delegated intent`. Do not publish program IDs, PDA seeds, exact account layouts, signer thresholds, wallet addresses or privileged operational procedures.
